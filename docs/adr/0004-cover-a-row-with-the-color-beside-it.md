# Cover a row with the color beside it

The old words on a translated row are covered by sampling the color just outside the row. When those samples agree, the row is filled with that color and the translation is drawn in black or white, shrunk to fit. When they disagree, the words sit on a picture, so the row stays as printed. The speaker can also mark that row kept in the upload preview.

A flat white rectangle was the simple alternative. It punches a hole through a colored title or a photo.
