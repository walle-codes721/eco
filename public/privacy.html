Privacy policy

Eco is a hackathon project by Lino Labs, built for the AMD Developer Hackathon ACT III. It translates talks and shop counters live. This page says what it stores and why. Eco has no ads and sells nothing about you.

If you listen to a talk or talk to a shop

  • You don't need an account. Eco stores no name or email for you.
  • Questions you type to a talk, and what you say or type at a shop counter, are stored with that talk or shop so the speaker or owner can see them, together with your chosen language.
  • Your phone keeps your language, your questions and your conversation id in its own browser storage. Eco sets no tracking cookies.

If you give a talk or run a shop

  • Google sign-in. If you sign in, Eco receives your name, email address, profile photo and Google account id from Google. It uses them only to keep your talks and shops in your account and to show you who is signed in. Eco does not see your Google password and asks for no other Google data.
  • Sign-in cookie. One cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. Signing out deletes it.
  • Your content. Your slides (PDF and text), notes and shop information are stored so Eco can show the slides and answer questions from them.
  • Your voice sample. Only if you tick the consent box, Eco stores a short recording of your voice and uses it to speak your talk in other languages in your voice. You can save one on your account page and reuse it for every talk, or record one for a single talk. You can delete it at any time from the page where you recorded it.

Pharmacies, clinics and hospitals

  • Please don't type personal health details (names, diagnoses, medicines, symptoms). Eco asks customers not to, but it can't stop them.
  • What you type is stored with the shop like any other message, and the owner can read it. It is sent to AMD Token Factory to be translated for the owner, and AMD logs prompts for about two days. For emergency and medical messages, the shop's own information is not sent along with it.
  • Eco does not delete conversations automatically yet: they stay until the owner or Lino Labs removes them. Eco is a hackathon demo and is not designed for real patient data.

Who processes the data

  • Cloudflare hosts Eco and stores the data above.
  • AMD Token Factory transcribes and translates speech and answers questions. It receives the audio of each spoken phrase and the relevant text. AMD logs prompts for about two days.
  • AMD Token Factory (OmniVoice) also receives your voice sample and the translated text to produce the spoken audio.
  • Google handles sign-in for speakers and shop owners.

How long it's kept

Your content stays until you ask us to delete it, or until the project ends. Email w72861313@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will remove your account and its content. You can delete your voice sample yourself at any time. Eco does not use your data to train models.
