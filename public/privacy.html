Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. Eco is a hackathon project by Lino Labs, run on a trial basis.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address, and nothing else from your Google account. We use them only to identify you and to put your name in your talk and shop links.

We also store what you create: talk slides and transcripts, shop sources and files, questions asked, and answers given. Listeners and customers who do not sign in are not identified.

Where it goes

To translate and answer, text you provide is sent to AMD Token Factory, which runs open models. AMD logs prompts for about two days under its own terms. Eco is hosted on Cloudflare. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Keeping and deleting

Content stays until you ask us to delete it. Email us from the address you signed in with and we will remove your account and its content.
