How it works

One call per phrase. Every language at once.

1

Hear

The browser cuts your speech at natural pauses. Each phrase goes to MiMo-V2.6-Flash on AMD Token Factory as audio.

2

Translate

One call returns the transcript and every language. Cost grows with languages, not with the size of the room.

3

Answer

Questions are matched locally against the slides or the shop's info, and Gemma-4-31B-IT answers only from those, citing where. Otherwise: "not covered".
